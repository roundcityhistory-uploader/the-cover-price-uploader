The Cover Price Uploader

A private tool that publishes videos to one YouTube channel.

This is a single-purpose application used by the owner of the YouTube channel The Cover Price to upload finished videos, captions and metadata to that channel.

It is not a product, not a service, and is not offered to anyone else. There is nothing to sign up for. It runs on the owner's own computer and acts only on the owner's own channel.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file to the owner's channel, as private, with a scheduled release time
  • Sets the title, description, tags and category
  • Uploads an English caption track
  • Sets the AI-content disclosure required by YouTube's policy
  • Reads back what it just uploaded, to confirm it was recorded correctly

What it does not do

  • It does not read, collect or process any other person's YouTube data
  • It does not read viewer comments, subscriber identities or personal information
  • It has no users other than the channel owner, and no sign-in for anyone else
